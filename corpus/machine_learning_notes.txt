Machine learning models learn patterns from historical data and use those patterns to make predictions on unseen examples. The quality of a model depends heavily on the quality and quantity of the training data available to it. Overfitting occurs when a model memorises the training set instead of learning general rules, which leads to poor performance on new and previously unseen data. Techniques such as cross-validation, regularisation, and early stopping help to reduce overfitting and improve how well a model generalises.
